# Better vehicle controls

**Vehicle driving improvements** changes how cars pull away, shift, reverse and steer from the
keyboard. It also lights the ground around your car at night. It's experimental and works in
single player only. It doesn't change any game files.

![The same road driven with the extension off and on](../media/steering.webp)

## 1. Turn it on

Open **Game extensions** and switch **Vehicle driving improvements** on. You can do it with
the game running.

The game takes the change at a safe moment. If the card says **To apply, resume the game, stop
the vehicle, release the accelerator, and turn off cruise control**, do just that.

[Screenshot: Game extensions, the Vehicle driving improvements card open with its four options]

## 2. Choose what you want

All four options start on. Turn off any you don't like, and that part drives as the game
normally does.

| Option | What you notice |
| --- | --- |
| **Natural acceleration and shifting** | Pulling away and shifting follow the engine speed and the gear |
| **Smooth reversing** | Reverse comes in gently and stays within the vehicle's own reverse speed |
| **Precise keyboard steering** | The wheel turns as long as you hold the key, even when the frame rate drops |
| **Light around the vehicle** | The ground around your car is lit while its headlights are on |

The light needs working headlights and a charged battery. It isn't written to your save.

![At night, the headlights switched off and on, and the light around the truck with them](../media/lights.webp)

## When it does nothing

- Acceleration and reversing only change for ordinary four-wheel vehicles with 3 to 5 gears
  and a running engine. Others drive as usual.
- Gamepad steering and towed vehicles keep the game's own steering.
- Other mods that change vehicle handling may not mix with it.
- **Compatibility and status** shows which game versions it supports. If it says **Outside
  supported range · Extension inactive**, the game version is newer than PZ Tools knows. Wait
  for a PZ Tools update, or turn on **Ignore supported version range** to try it anyway.
- **The extension could not be applied. Check the logs for details.** means it failed to
  start in the game. The **Logs** page has the reason.

## Turning it off

Switch it off and the game's own driving comes back at once.
